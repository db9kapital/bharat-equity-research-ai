## 3️⃣ COMPETITIVE MOAT ANALYSIS

### Moat Type Identified
**Primary**: Switching Costs + Brand Moat + Scale

- **Switching Costs** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  - Clients depend on TCS for critical systems
  - Cost of switching to another vendor = 3-6 months work
  - High integration with client operations
  
- **Brand Moat** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
  - Trusted by Fortune 500 companies globally
  - Reputation for quality and reliability
  - 25 years of consistent delivery
  
- **Scale Moat** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
  - Largest IT services company in India
  - Can take on larger, complex projects
  - Better pricing power with clients
  - Better negotiating power with suppliers

**Overall Moat Strength**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5 - VERY STRONG)

### Moat Durability (10-20 Year Outlook)

**DURABLE** - Multiple factors protect this moat:

1. **Network Effects**: More projects = more expertise = better service = more projects
2. **High Switching Costs**: Will persist as long as digital services are critical
3. **Continuous Innovation**: Investing heavily in AI, cloud, blockchain
4. **Global Footprint**: 25+ delivery centers, hard to replicate
5. **Talent Moat**: Access to 600,000+ technical talent pool (India advantage)

**Threats**:
- ⚠️ **AI Disruption**: Automation could replace some services (but TCS adapts faster)
- ⚠️ **Wage Inflation**: Rising salaries in India could compress margins
- ⚠️ **Nearshoring**: Clients moving work closer (but TCS expanding globally)

**Verdict**: Moat likely sustainable for 15+ years. Strong.
