## 🚨 RED FLAGS - When Macro Mood Darkens

**If ANY of these happen, shift to DEFENSIVE:**

```
🚨 CRITICAL RED FLAGS:

1. FPI OUTFLOWS FOR 3+ MONTHS STRAIGHT
   Signal: Foreign investors exiting India
   Action: REDUCE positions, move to quality/cash
   
2. GDP GROWTH SLIPS BELOW 5%
   Signal: Economy slowing sharply
   Action: AVOID cyclicals, stick to defensive
   
3. RUPEE WEAKENS SHARPLY (INR > 85/USD)
   Signal: Currency crisis forming
   Action: Avoid import-dependent sectors
   
4. IIP TURNS NEGATIVE FOR 2+ MONTHS
   Signal: Manufacturing collapsing
   Action: Avoid industrial/auto stocks
   
5. BANK DEPOSITS GROWING < LOAN GROWTH
   Signal: Liquidity stress forming
   Action: Reduce bank positions
   
6. CORPORATE EARNINGS TURN NEGATIVE
   Signal: Profit cycle breaking down
   Action: Move to cash/ultra-defensive
   
7. RBI RATE HIKES SIGNAL (Inflation rising)
   Signal: Interest rate cycle turning up
   Action: Avoid high-leverage companies
```
