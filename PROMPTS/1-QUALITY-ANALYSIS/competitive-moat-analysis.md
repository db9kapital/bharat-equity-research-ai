# Competitive Moat Analysis Prompt

**Charlie Munger Philosophy**: "The difference between a good business and a great business is the moat."

## Prompt for AI Models

---

I'm analyzing **[COMPANY NAME]** listed on **[NSE/BSE]** for investment purposes using Charlie Munger's framework.

Please help me evaluate the company's competitive moats (economic advantages). Analyze the following:

### 1. **Identify the Moat Type**
   - **Brand moat**: Does the company have pricing power? (Examples: ITC, Nestlé India)
   - **Cost moat**: Is it a low-cost producer? (Examples: Reliance, Bajaj)
   - **Network moat**: Does value increase with users? (Examples: NCDEX, Stock exchanges)
   - **Switching cost moat**: High cost for customers to switch? (Examples: TCS, Infosys for enterprise IT)
   - **Regulatory moat**: Protected by regulations? (Examples: Banks, Telecom companies)

### 2. **Moat Durability** (Critical for Munger)
   - How sustainable is this moat for the next 10-20 years?
   - Is technology disruption a threat?
   - Could new entrants easily replicate the advantage?

### 3. **Financial Evidence of Moat**
   - Compare **Gross Profit Margins** vs industry peers over last 5 years
   - Check **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)** vs Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
   - Analyze **market share trends** - is it growing or shrinking?
   - Review **pricing power**: Can they raise prices without losing customers?

### 4. **Competitive Position**
   - Who are the main competitors?
   - How does this company compare on key metrics?
   - Is the moat widening or narrowing?

### 5. **Moat Strength Rating**
   - ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very Strong) - Decades of protection
   - ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Strong) - 10-20 years of protection
   - ⭐⭐⭐ (Moderate) - 5-10 years of protection
   - ⭐⭐ (Weak) - Less than 5 years
   - ⭐ (No moat) - Commodity business

### 6. **Investment Implication**
   - Does a strong moat justify a higher valuation?
   - At what price would this moat be attractive?
