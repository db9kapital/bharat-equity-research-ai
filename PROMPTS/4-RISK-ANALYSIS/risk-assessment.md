# Comprehensive Risk Assessment Prompt

**Charlie Munger Warning**: "The three most important words in investing are 'margin of safety.'"

Risk assessment is critical before deploying capital.

## Prompt for AI Models

---

I'm analyzing the **risks** associated with investing in **[COMPANY NAME]** (NSE/BSE: [TICKER]).

Please perform a comprehensive risk assessment covering:

### 1. **Financial Risks** (Critical)

**Leverage & Solvency Risk**
- Current debt-to-equity ratio vs industry average
- Trend over last 5 years (increasing/decreasing)
- Interest coverage ratio (ability to pay interest)
- Refinancing risk: When do major debts mature?
- Any covenant breaches or default risks?

**Liquidity Risk**
- Current ratio & quick ratio
- Working capital trends
- Cash burn rate (if unprofitable)
- Ability to meet short-term obligations

**Earnings Quality**
- One-time gains/losses in recent quarters
- Changes in accounting policies
- Related party transactions
- Potential for earnings manipulation

### 2. **Business & Operational Risks** (Important)

**Revenue & Market Risk**
- Customer concentration: Top 10 customers = what % of revenue?
- Product/service concentration risk
- Market size and growth rate
- Susceptibility to economic downturns

**Competitive Risk**
- New competitors entering market
- Price competition pressure
- Technology disruption threat
- Market share erosion trends

**Operational Risk**
- Supply chain concentration
- Key person dependencies
- Quality or compliance issues
- Geographic concentration (if relevant)

**Regulatory & Compliance Risk**
- Regulatory changes affecting business
- Litigation or legal cases pending
- Environmental compliance
- Tax-related risks

### 3. **Industry & Macro Risks** (Contextual)

**Industry Headwinds**
- Cyclicality of industry
- Long-term industry growth prospects
- Structural decline in sector?
- Government policy changes affecting sector

**Macro Economic Risk**
- Interest rate sensitivity
- Currency risk (if exports/imports significant)
- Inflation impact on margins
- Economic slowdown impact

### 4. **Management & Governance Risk** (Character Test)

**Management Track Record**
- Past decisions: Wise or foolish capital allocation?
- History of hitting/missing guidance
- Insider selling vs buying patterns
- Succession plan clarity

**Governance Issues**
- Any regulatory actions or investigations?
- Related party transaction concerns?
- Corporate governance violations?
- Audit qualifications or red flags?

---

## Risk Scoring System

**Rate each risk category: 1-10 scale**

- **1-2**: Minimal risk - Negligible impact on investment
- **3-4**: Low risk - Manageable, well-understood
- **5-6**: Moderate risk - Concerning but mitigable
- **7-8**: High risk - Significant threat to thesis
- **9-10**: Critical risk - Investment decision reversing

---

## Risk Mitigation Strategy

For each identified risk, specify:
- **Impact**: High / Medium / Low
- **Probability**: High / Medium / Low
- **Mitigation**: How can you reduce this risk?

Example:
| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| Customer concentration (Top 3 = 60%) | High | High | Monitor customer relationships, diversification plan |
| Rising competition | High | Medium | Monitor market share, pricing power |
| Debt maturity | Medium | Low | Monitor refinancing rates, cash positions |

---

## Deal-Breaker Risks

Are there any risks that would automatically disqualify this stock?

**Examples of deal-breakers:**
- ❌ Ongoing regulatory investigation
- ❌ Accounting fraud or misrepresentation
- ❌ Debt default or covenant breach
- ❌ Management with poor track record
- ❌ Technology disruption threat (for some sectors)
- ❌ Deteriorating business fundamentals

---

## Overall Risk Rating

**Sum up risks into an overall investment risk profile:**

- **🟢 GREEN** (Low Risk): Well-understood business, manageable risks, good management
- **🟡 YELLOW** (Moderate Risk): Some concerns, but generally acceptable
- **🔴 RED** (High Risk): Significant risks, requires deep conviction and margin of safety

---

## Munger's Risk Management Principle

"If you're going to do something, you should make sure you're not going to lose money."

**Key Questions:**
1. What could go wrong? (Worst case scenario)
2. How would you know if your thesis is breaking down?
3. What's your exit strategy if risks materialize?
4. Is your margin of safety sufficient given these risks?

## Usage Notes
- Works on: All AI models
- Data needed: Annual reports, quarterly filings, news articles
- Time: 20-30 minutes
- Review quarterly for emerging risks
- Update before buying/selling decisions
